Story of my art

Michael Wagner is a true surrealist for the modern age. By intertwining the timeless depth of classical compositions with vibrant psychedelic elements, he reimagines themes of religion and enlightenment with striking originality. His works bring a refreshing vitality to the often conventional landscape of contemporary art.

After years of exploring religion and philosophy, Wagner masterfully translates the profound questions of life and spirituality into visual form. Rejecting traditional boundaries, he demonstrates that the "box" we imagine confining us was actually never there. By seamlessly merging the conscious and subconscious, his art offers a divine glimpse into the dream state and the mysteries of the afterlife. These colorful, thought-provoking pieces ignite the viewer's inner creativity, evoking a sense of lucid dreaming for all who lay eyes on them.
